## Body
This is an internal enterprise platform that runs the full project lifecycle for an engineering services company: from budget planning and project assignment, through contracts and subcontractor procurement, to expenses, cash advances, and project closure.

I joined the frontend team in 2024 and focused on the monitoring area, building dashboards for contracts, subcontractors, overhead, financials, progress, and QA/QC, plus an integration monitor that lets operators filter failed syncs and retry them individually or in bulk.

Alongside that, I worked through multi-step approval workflows across finance and project modules and closed a large share of UAT findings ahead of release.

Built with Vue 3 and Vite, state in Vuex, UI on Tailwind CSS and Radix Vue, charts with ApexCharts.

Internal enterprise app — screenshots use sanitized data; source code and live access are not public.
